Research and professional experience
======

  * Build data-collection and analysis workflows that integrate multi-camera video, sensor, phenotype, and genetic data for precision poultry management.

* **Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant**, School of Computing, University of Georgia — 2022–2025
  * Conducted research in AI, computer vision, health informatics, and systems; contributed to projects involving efficient language-model inference and DNS-log analysis.

* **Linux Expert**, Parspack Cloud Services, Tehran, Iran — 2021–2022
  * Resolved customer issues involving Linux servers, cloud hosting, VPS environments, and related infrastructure.
