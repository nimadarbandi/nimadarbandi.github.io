I am excited to share that **“Patient Co-Pilot: A Source-Grounded Companion App for Navigating Complex Care”** has been accepted to the **Demonstration Paper Track of GenAI4Health 2026, a NeurIPS 2026 workshop**.

This workshop acceptance is a meaningful milestone for Patient Co-Pilot. The app brings together medical records, visit conversations, and care-related documents to help patients understand their care context, prepare for appointments, and ask informed questions grounded in their own sources.

I am grateful to the GenAI4Health program chairs and reviewers for the opportunity to share this work with the NeurIPS workshop community. I look forward to preparing the camera-ready paper and presenting the demonstration.

The [OpenReview submission page](https://openreview.net/forum?id=xCCYsAUZMg) has the decision, reviews, and meta-review. To learn more about the app, read my posts on the [original Patient Co-Pilot workflow](/posts/2026/04/patient-copilot-practical/) and the [Patient Co-Pilot v2 release](/posts/2026/04/patient-copilot-v2-release/).
